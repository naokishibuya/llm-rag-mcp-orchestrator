# Countries and Landmarks

## Asia

- **Japan**: An archipelago of 6,852 islands. Mount Fuji (3,776 m) is the tallest peak, located about 100 km southwest of Tokyo. Japan has over 1,500 earthquakes per year and more vending machines per capita than any other country (about 1 per 23 people).
- **India**: The world's most populous country with over 1.4 billion people. The Taj Mahal in Agra took 22 years to build (1632-1653) and was constructed by approximately 20,000 workers. India has 22 officially recognized languages.
- **China**: Home to the Great Wall (21,196 km total length) and the Terracotta Army. The Yangtze River (6,300 km) is Asia's longest river. China's high-speed rail network is the world's largest at over 42,000 km.

## Europe

- **France**: The most visited country in the world with about 90 million tourists per year. The Eiffel Tower was built for the 1889 World's Fair and was originally planned to be dismantled after 20 years. France has over 1,600 types of cheese.
- **Italy**: Contains more UNESCO World Heritage Sites (59) than any other country. The Colosseum in Rome could hold 50,000 spectators. Venice is built on 118 small islands connected by over 400 bridges.
- **Iceland**: Sits on the Mid-Atlantic Ridge between the North American and Eurasian tectonic plates. It has over 130 volcanoes and produces nearly 100% of its electricity from renewable sources (geothermal and hydroelectric).

## Americas

- **Brazil**: Contains about 60% of the Amazon Rainforest, which produces roughly 6% of the world's oxygen. The Amazon River has over 1,100 tributaries. Brazil is the 5th largest country by both area and population.
- **United States**: The Grand Canyon is up to 1,800 m deep. Yellowstone National Park sits atop a supervolcano. The country spans 6 time zones (including Alaska and Hawaii).
- **Peru**: Home to Machu Picchu, the 15th-century Inca citadel located 2,430 m above sea level. It was unknown to the outside world until American historian Hiram Bingham brought it to international attention in 1911.

## Africa

- **Egypt**: The Great Pyramid of Giza is the only surviving Ancient Wonder of the World. The Nile River (6,650 km) is the longest river in Africa and one of the longest in the world.
- **South Africa**: Home to Table Mountain in Cape Town, one of the oldest mountains in the world (approximately 600 million years old). South Africa has three capital cities: Pretoria, Cape Town, and Bloemfontein.
- **Madagascar**: The world's 4th largest island, with about 90% of its wildlife found nowhere else on Earth, including over 100 species of lemurs.
